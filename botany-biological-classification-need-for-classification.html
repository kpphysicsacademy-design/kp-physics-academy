Biological Classification • Concept Lesson

Need for Classification

Main concept: Classification is the systematic grouping of organisms based on similarities, differences and relationships. It makes the enormous diversity of life easier to study.

📚 Why Classification is Needed

Classification organises millions of organisms into manageable groups. It supports identification, comparison, scientific naming and the study of natural and evolutionary relationships.
