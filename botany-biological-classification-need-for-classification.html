Lesson 1 • Biological Classification

1️⃣ Need for Classification

Classification is the systematic arrangement of organisms into groups based on similarities, differences and relationships. Because millions of organisms exist on Earth, studying every organism separately would be difficult. Classification makes biological diversity easier to understand.

🌍 Why is Classification Needed?

1. Easy study: It organises a huge number of organisms into manageable groups.
2. Identification: Scientists can identify organisms by comparing their characteristics with known groups.
3. Naming: Classification supports systematic naming and avoids confusion caused by different local names.
4. Relationships: Similarities and differences help us understand relationships among organisms.
5. Biodiversity: It helps organise and study the enormous diversity of life on Earth.

🔬 Basis of Classification

Organisms may be compared using features such as:

• Cell type and cell structure
• Body organisation
• Mode of nutrition
• Reproduction
• Evolutionary and phylogenetic relationships

🧠 NEET Key Points

✓ Classification means systematic grouping of organisms.
✓ It makes the study of biodiversity organised and convenient.
✓ Modern classification considers natural and evolutionary relationships.
✓ Classification, identification and nomenclature are closely connected in biological systematics.
