Class 11 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Formula-first revision • Competitive foundation

📏 Units & Dimensions

[v] = LT⁻¹
[a] = LT⁻²
[F] = MLT⁻²

Use dimensional analysis to check equations and derive relations up to dimensionless constants.

🏃 Kinematics

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as
v⃗ = dr⃗/dt

⚖️ Laws of Motion

ΣF⃗ = m a⃗
p⃗ = m v⃗
Impulse = Δp

Draw a free-body diagram before applying Newton's laws. Action and reaction act on different bodies.

🔋 Work, Energy & Power

W = ∫F⃗·dr⃗
K = ½mv²
P = dW/dt
Spring energy = ½kx²

🔄 Circular & Rotational Motion

v = rω
a_c = v²/r = rω²
τ = Iα
K_rot = ½Iω²

🌍 Gravitation

F = Gm₁m₂/r²
g = GM/R²
v_escape = √(2GM/R)
v_orbit = √(GM/r)

🌡️ Thermodynamics

ΔQ = ΔU + W
Ideal gas: PV = nRT
Efficiency = W/Q_in

For an ideal gas, internal energy depends only on temperature. Keep sign convention consistent for work.

〰️ Oscillations & Waves

x = A sin(ωt + φ)
ω = 2πf
T_spring = 2π√(m/k)
T_pendulum = 2π√(l/g)
