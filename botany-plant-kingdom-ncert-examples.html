Concept 10: NCERT Examples & NEET Identification

Memorise representative examples together with their diagnostic characteristics.

  • Algae examples: Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Ulothrix, Spirogyra, Sargassum and Polysiphonia.
  • Bryophyte examples: Riccia, Marchantia and Funaria.
  • Pteridophyte examples: Psilotum, Lycopodium, Selaginella, Equisetum and Pteris.
  • Gymnosperm examples: Cycas, Pinus and Gnetum.
  • Use diagnostic features rather than memorising names alone.

NEET Identification Guide

  • Green algae: Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Ulothrix and Spirogyra.
  • Brown algae: Ectocarpus, Dictyota, Laminaria and Sargassum.
  • Red algae: Polysiphonia, Porphyra and Gracilaria.
  • Bryophytes: Riccia and Marchantia are liverworts; Funaria is a moss.
  • Pteridophytes: Psilotum, Lycopodium, Selaginella, Equisetum and Pteris.
  • Gymnosperms: Cycas, Pinus and Gnetum are major NCERT examples.

High-Yield NEET Clues

  • Selaginella is important for heterospory and seed-habit evolution.
  • Funaria is used to identify a typical moss.
  • Marchantia is a common liverwort example.
  • Pinus and Cycas represent gymnosperms with naked seeds.
  • Examples should be learned together with their diagnostic group features.

Practice

Use NEET identification clues, then test yourself one question at a time. Incorrect answers should return until correctly mastered.
