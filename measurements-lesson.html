Lesson 1: Measurements 📏

Learn the basic idea, use a simple formula, solve an example, then prove your understanding.

1. Learn the concept

Measurement means comparing an unknown physical quantity with a known standard unit.

Examples of physical quantities include length, mass and time. A measurement should be written with a number and a unit, such as 5 m or 10 s.

2. Formula focus

For this first lesson, we will use speed as a simple measurement relationship.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

v = s / t

3. Worked example

Example: A student travels 20 m in 5 s. What is the speed?

Speed = 20 ÷ 5 = 4 m/s.

4. Prove your understanding

A cyclist travels 30 m in 10 s. What is the speed?
