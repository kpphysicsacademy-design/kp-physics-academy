STEP 1 • LEARN

What is measurement?

Measurement means comparing an unknown physical quantity with a known standard unit.

Every measurement needs a number + unit, such as 5 m, 10 s or 2 kg.

STEP 2 • DISCOVER

Speed connects distance and time

Speed tells us how much distance an object covers in a given amount of time.

v = s ÷ t

ttime
STEP 3 • THINK

Predict before calculating

Example: A student travels 20 m in 5 s.

Using v = s ÷ t, the speed is 4 m/s.
