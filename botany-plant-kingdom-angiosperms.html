🌸 CONCEPT 6 • PLANT KINGDOM

Angiosperms

Flowering plants with enclosed ovules, fruits and the unique process of double fertilisation.

🎯 Learning Objective

Understand the defining features of angiosperms and explain flowers, pollination, double fertilisation, seed and fruit formation.

🖼️ Visual Learning

Visual: The flower is the reproductive structure of angiosperms.

📖 Simple Explanation

Angiosperms are flowering plants. Their ovules are enclosed inside an ovary. After fertilisation, ovules become seeds and the ovary usually develops into a fruit. This is the major difference from gymnosperms, whose seeds are not enclosed in fruits.

🔑 Key Characteristics

  • Flowers are the reproductive structures.
  • Ovules are enclosed within the ovary.
  • Seeds develop within fruits.
  • They show double fertilisation.
  • The dominant plant body is the diploid sporophyte.

🌱 Flower and Gametophytes

♂ Male side

Anther produces pollen grains. The pollen grain represents the highly reduced male gametophyte.

♀ Female side

Ovule contains the embryo sac, the highly reduced female gametophyte.

🧬 Double Fertilisation: Step by Step

POLLINATION → POLLEN TUBE → TWO MALE GAMETES → SYNGAMY + TRIPLE FUSION → EMBRYO + ENDOSPERM
  1. Pollen reaches the stigma and germinates.
  2. A pollen tube grows through the style towards the ovule.
  3. One male gamete fuses with the egg: syngamy, producing the zygote.
  4. The second male gamete fuses with the two polar nuclei: triple fusion, producing the primary endosperm nucleus.
  5. Syngamy and triple fusion together are called double fertilisation.

🧩 Monocots vs Dicots

Monocots

One cotyledon; examples include wheat and maize.

Dicots

Two cotyledons; examples include pea and bean.

⭐ Important Examples

Wheat, maize, rice, pea, bean, mango and sunflower are angiosperms.

⚠️ NEET Traps

  • Double fertilisation is characteristic of angiosperms.
  • Fruit develops mainly from the ovary after fertilisation.
  • Endosperm formation is linked with triple fusion.
  • Angiosperm seeds are enclosed within fruits.

💡 Example-Based Thinking

Question: A plant produces flowers and fruits, and two fertilisation events occur inside the embryo sac. Identify the group.

Answer: Angiosperms, because they show double fertilisation and have enclosed ovules.

📝 Quick Recap

Flower → enclosed ovule → pollination → double fertilisation → seed + endosperm → fruit.
