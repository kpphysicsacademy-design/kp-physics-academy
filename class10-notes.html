Class 10 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Quick revision notes • Board-exam focused

🔭 Light: Reflection

∠i = ∠r
Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
Magnification m = −v/u

For a spherical mirror, f = R/2. Sign convention must be applied consistently.

🔍 Light: Refraction

n = sin i / sin r
Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
Lens magnification m = v/u

Refractive index compares the speed of light in vacuum with that in a medium: n = c/v.

👁️ Human Eye

Near point of normal eye ≈ 25 cm
Myopia → concave lens
Hypermetropia → convex lens

The eye lens changes focal length by accommodation. Presbyopia is associated with reduced accommodation with age.

⚡ Electricity

I = Q/t
V = IR
P = VI = I²R = V²/R
Electrical energy = Pt

🧲 Magnetic Effects

Current-carrying conductor produces magnetic field
Motor: electrical → mechanical energy
Generator: mechanical → electrical energy

Electromagnetic induction is the production of current due to changing magnetic field through a circuit.

🔋 Sources of Energy

Renewable: solar, wind, hydro
Non-renewable: coal, petroleum, natural gas

A good energy source should provide useful energy efficiently, be economical and have manageable environmental impact.
