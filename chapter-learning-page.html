Concepts

Explore the main ideas introduced in The Wonderful World of Science. Each concept follows the chapter's journey from curiosity to scientific investigation.

1What is Science?Science is a way of thinking, observing and doing things to understand the world and uncover the secrets of the universe. 2Curiosity and DiscoveryEvery discovery can lead to more questions and more things to find out. 3Science is EverywhereExplore mountains, oceans, forests, food, materials, water, weather and outer space. 4Living Things and Our PlanetPlants and animals survive and thrive in different regions, while Earth provides the environment that supports life. 5Materials, Food and WaterAsk what everyday things are made of, how materials differ and how water changes with heating and cooling. 6The Scientific MethodObserve → Question → Guess → Test → Analyse. 7Thinking Like a ScientistAnyone asking questions and finding answers can work like a scientist. 8Joyful ExplorationKeep observing, wondering, asking “Why?” and discovering together.

Examples

Learn science by following a simple investigation from observation to conclusion.

👀
Observe

A potted plant near a window bends toward the sunlight.

❓
Question

Why is the plant growing toward one side?

💭
Think

Maybe the plant is responding to the direction of light.

🧪
Test

Observe similar plants placed in different light directions and record changes.

💡
Result

Use your observations to decide whether the evidence supports the idea.

Key Takeaways

Be curious. Observe carefully. Ask questions. Test ideas using evidence. Learn from results. Science is a joyful journey of exploration, and discovery often leads to even more questions.
