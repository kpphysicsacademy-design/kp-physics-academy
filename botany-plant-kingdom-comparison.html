Comparative Features of Plant Groups

Explanation

Compare major plant groups using the features most frequently tested in NEET.

  • Algae: simple thalloid body; no true roots, stems or leaves.
  • Bryophytes: dominant gametophyte; water is required for fertilisation.
  • Pteridophytes: first vascular cryptogams; dominant sporophyte.
  • Gymnosperms: naked seeds and exposed ovules.
  • Angiosperms: flowers, enclosed ovules, fruits and double fertilisation.
