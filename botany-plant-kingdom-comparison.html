📖 Simple Explanation

Plant groups can be compared using a few powerful clues. Algae are largely aquatic and simple. Bryophytes lack vascular tissue and have dominant gametophytes. Pteridophytes have vascular tissue but no seeds. Gymnosperms produce naked seeds. Angiosperms produce flowers and fruits.
