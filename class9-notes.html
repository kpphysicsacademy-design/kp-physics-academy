Class 9 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Quick revision notes • Formula-first revision

📐 Motion

Speed = Distance / Time
Velocity = Displacement / Time
a = (v − u) / t

Distance is scalar; displacement is vector. Speed is scalar; velocity is vector.

🚗 Equations of Motion

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Use these equations for uniformly accelerated motion along a straight line.

⚖️ Force & Laws

F = ma
Momentum p = mv

Newton I: inertia. Newton II: force changes momentum; for constant mass F = ma. Newton III: action and reaction are equal and opposite.

🌍 Gravitation

F = Gm₁m₂/r²
Weight W = mg

Mass is constant for a body; weight depends on gravitational field strength. g near Earth is about 9.8 m/s².

🔋 Work & Energy

W = F s cosθ
K.E. = ½mv²
P.E. near Earth = mgh
Power = Work / Time

🔊 Sound

v = fλ
T = 1/f

Frequency determines pitch. Amplitude is related to loudness. Echo is caused by reflection of sound.
