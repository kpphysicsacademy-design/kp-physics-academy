Class 6 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Quick revision notes • Physics only

📏 Measurement

Length → metre (m)
Mass → kilogram (kg)
Time → second (s)

Use standard units. Measure length with a ruler or measuring scale. Avoid parallax error by keeping the eye vertically above the mark.

🏃 Motion

Motion = change of position with time
Distance = total path travelled

A body can be at rest relative to one object and moving relative to another. Common motions: rectilinear, circular and periodic.

💡 Light

Light travels in straight lines in a uniform medium
Reflection: light returns from a surface

Luminous objects produce light. Non-luminous objects are seen when light reflected from them enters our eyes. Transparent materials transmit most light.

⚡ Electric Circuits

Cell → source of electrical energy
Closed circuit → current can flow

A switch opens or closes a circuit. Conductors allow current to pass easily; insulators generally do not.

🧲 Magnets

Like poles repel
Unlike poles attract

A magnet has north and south poles. Magnetic force is strongest near the poles. A freely suspended magnet points approximately north–south.

🔥 Heat & Temperature

Heat = energy transferred because of temperature difference
Temperature indicates degree of hotness/coldness

Heat can transfer by conduction, convection and radiation. Metals are generally good conductors of heat.
