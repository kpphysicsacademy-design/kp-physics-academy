Class 7 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Quick revision notes • Physics only

⏱️ Motion & Time

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
SI unit of speed = m/s

Uniform motion covers equal distances in equal intervals of time. A simple pendulum can be used to measure time intervals.

🌡️ Heat

Temperature → degree of hotness
Clinical thermometer → body temperature

Heat flows from a hotter body to a colder body. Conduction is common in solids; convection in fluids; radiation does not require a medium.

⚡ Electric Current

Electric circuit = closed conducting path
Heating effect → current can produce heat

A fuse protects a circuit by melting when excessive current flows. Electric current can also produce magnetic effects.

💡 Light

Angle of incidence = angle of reflection
Plane mirror → virtual, erect image

A plane mirror forms an image at the same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front. Regular reflection occurs from smooth surfaces.

🌬️ Pressure

Pressure = Force ÷ Area
Smaller area → greater pressure for same force

Sharp knives cut easily because force acts over a small area. Liquids and gases exert pressure.

🌊 Sound

Sound needs a material medium
Vibrating object → source of sound

Loudness is related to amplitude. Pitch is related to frequency. Sound cannot travel through vacuum.
