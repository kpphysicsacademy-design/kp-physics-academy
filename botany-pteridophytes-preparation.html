🌿 Pteridophytes Preparation

Learn the core concepts first, then complete an initial practice before taking the full 50-question mastery test.

📚 Concepts to prepare

1. Position in Plant KingdomSeedless vascular plants; called vascular cryptogams.
2. Plant bodyDominant independent sporophyte with true roots, stem and leaves.
3. Vascular tissuesXylem and phloem distinguish pteridophytes from bryophytes.
4. Sporangia and sporophyllsSpores are produced in sporangia, often borne on sporophylls; clusters are called sori.
5. GametophyteUsually a small, haploid, free-living prothallus.
6. Sexual reproductionAntheridia and archegonia produce gametes; water is required for fertilisation.
7. Life cycleSporophyte → spores → gametophyte → gametes → zygote → sporophyte.
8. Homospory and heterosporyHeterosporous forms produce microspores and megaspores.
9. Seed habitHeterospory is an important evolutionary step towards seed habit.
10. NEET examplesLycopodium, Selaginella, Salvinia, Equisetum, Pteris and Adiantum.
