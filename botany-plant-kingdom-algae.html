Algae

General characteristics, classification, pigments, reproduction and economic importance — built around core NCERT concepts.

🎯 Learning Objective

Understand what algae are, compare the three major classes and identify their pigments, stored food, habitat, reproduction and important examples.

📖 What are algae?

Algae are simple, chlorophyll-bearing, autotrophic and largely aquatic organisms. Their body is generally thalloid and their forms range from unicellular and colonial organisms to filamentous and large marine forms.

Chlorophyll-bearingAutotrophicMostly aquaticThalloid body

🔑 Reproduction at a glance

  • Vegetative: commonly by fragmentation.
  • Asexual: by spores; motile zoospores are important in many forms.
  • Sexual: fusion of gametes; patterns include isogamy, anisogamy and oogamy.

🎨 Three major classes

🟢 ChlorophyceaeGreen algae
🟤 PhaeophyceaeBrown algae
🔴 RhodophyceaeRed algae

💡 Economic importance

  • Major primary producers in aquatic ecosystems.
  • Important in carbon dioxide fixation and oxygen availability in aquatic environments.
  • Some species are used as food and protein-rich supplements.
  • Commercial products include algin, carrageen and agar from specific marine algae.

⚠️ NEET traps to remember

  • Green ≠ only freshwater: habitat alone is not enough for classification.
  • Pigment + stored food are high-value comparison points.
  • Red algae: floridean starch.
  • Brown algae: laminarin and mannitol.
