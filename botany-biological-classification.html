🦠 Biological Classification

Learn the NCERT concepts systematically before moving to practice and chapter tests.

📖 Concepts & Learn

Learning sequence: Need for Classification → Five Kingdom Classification → Monera → Protista → Fungi → Viruses, Viroids & Lichens.

1. Need for Classification

Classification organises the enormous diversity of organisms into meaningful groups, making identification, comparison and systematic study easier.

2. Five Kingdom Classification

Whittaker's system classifies organisms into Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia using criteria such as cell structure, body organisation, mode of nutrition, reproduction and phylogenetic relationships.

3. Kingdom Monera

Monerans are prokaryotic organisms. Important groups include archaebacteria, eubacteria and cyanobacteria. Study their cell organisation, nutrition and reproduction.

4. Kingdom Protista

Protists are mainly unicellular eukaryotes. Major groups include chrysophytes, dinoflagellates, euglenoids, slime moulds and protozoans.

5. Kingdom Fungi

Fungi are heterotrophic organisms that generally possess chitinous cell walls. Learn the major groups and their modes of reproduction.

6. Viruses

Viruses are acellular infectious agents containing either DNA or RNA enclosed in a protein coat and depend on host cells for multiplication.

7. Viroids

Viroids are infectious agents made of small naked RNA molecules without a protein coat.

8. Lichens

Lichens are symbiotic associations between an alga and a fungus. The algal partner provides food while the fungal partner provides shelter and absorbs water and minerals.
