Class 8 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Quick revision notes • Physics only

💪 Force & Pressure

Pressure = Force ÷ Area
SI unit of pressure = pascal (Pa)

Force can change motion, direction or shape. Pressure increases when the same force acts on a smaller area.

🧱 Friction

Friction opposes relative motion
Rolling friction is generally less than sliding friction

Friction is useful for walking and braking but also causes wear and energy loss. Lubrication can reduce friction.

🔊 Sound

Frequency = number of vibrations per second
Unit of frequency = hertz (Hz)

Amplitude affects loudness; frequency affects pitch. Human hearing is approximately 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

💡 Light

Reflection: ∠i = ∠r
White light can split into colours

The human eye detects visible light. A periscope uses reflection. Dispersion separates white light into its component colours.

⚡ Electricity

Current flows through a closed circuit
Fuse → safety device

Conductors such as metals allow current to flow. Excess current can heat wires and create fire risk; fuses help protect circuits.

🌍 Force & Motion Basics

Balanced forces → no change in state of motion
Unbalanced force → changes motion

A force may start, stop, speed up, slow down or change the direction of motion.
