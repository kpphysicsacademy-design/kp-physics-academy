Five Kingdom Classification

R. H. Whittaker proposed the five-kingdom system: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia. Major criteria include cell structure, body organisation, mode of nutrition, reproduction and evolutionary relationships.

Key Points

Understand the NCERT definition and keywords.
Compare this concept with related biological groups.
Revise important examples and differences for NEET questions.
