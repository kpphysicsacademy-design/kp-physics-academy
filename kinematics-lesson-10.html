🧠 Learn

Two-dimensional motion is handled by resolving motion into perpendicular components. Apply the kinematic equations independently along each axis when acceleration is known, then combine components to obtain displacement or velocity.

Key idea: x- and y-components can be solved separately; vectors are combined only after their components are known.
