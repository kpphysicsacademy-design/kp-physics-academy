Lesson 3 • Biological Classification

Kingdom Monera

Main concept: Kingdom Monera includes prokaryotic organisms. They lack a membrane-bound nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. Bacteria, cyanobacteria and archaebacteria are important members.

📚 Learn Monera in Detail

🔬 1. Prokaryotic Cell Organisation
Monerans are prokaryotes. Their genetic material is not enclosed by a nuclear membrane. They also lack membrane-bound organelles such as mitochondria, Golgi bodies and chloroplasts.
🦠 2. Bacteria
Bacteria are among the most abundant organisms on Earth and occur in soil, water, air and inside other organisms. They may be autotrophic or heterotrophic.
🔵 3. Bacterial Shapes
Common bacterial forms include coccus (spherical), bacillus (rod-shaped), vibrio (comma-shaped) and spirillum (spiral).
🌋 4. Archaebacteria
Archaebacteria are known for surviving in extreme habitats. Important types include halophiles, thermoacidophiles and methanogens. Methanogens occur in the rumen of cattle and help produce methane.
🌿 5. Cyanobacteria
Cyanobacteria are photosynthetic prokaryotes, commonly called blue-green algae. Examples include Nostoc and Anabaena. Some can fix atmospheric nitrogen.
🍽️ 6. Nutrition
Monerans show great nutritional diversity. They may be photosynthetic autotrophs, chemosynthetic autotrophs or heterotrophs such as saprophytes and parasites.
🔁 7. Reproduction
Bacteria mainly reproduce by binary fission. Under unfavourable conditions some form resistant spores. Genetic recombination can occur through processes such as conjugation, transformation and transduction.
