🌿 CONCEPT 1

Basis of Classification

🎯 Learning Objective

Understand how major plant groups are classified using body organisation, vascular tissue and reproductive structures.

📖 Simple Explanation

Major plant groups are compared using body organisation, presence or absence of vascular tissue, seed formation and reproductive structures.

  • Algae: simple thalloid body
  • Bryophytes: non-vascular plants
  • Pteridophytes: vascular plants reproducing by spores
  • Gymnosperms: seed plants with naked seeds
  • Angiosperms: flowering plants with seeds enclosed in fruits

🔑 Key Concepts

  • Vascular tissue distinguishes bryophytes from pteridophytes and seed plants.
  • Seed formation distinguishes seed plants from spore-producing groups.
  • Gymnosperms have naked seeds; angiosperms have ovules enclosed in an ovary.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Do not confuse pteridophytes with seed plants: pteridophytes are vascular but reproduce by spores.

📝 Quick Recap

Algae → Bryophytes → Pteridophytes → Gymnosperms → Angiosperms is the broad study sequence used here.
