🌿 CONCEPT 1 • PLANT KINGDOM

Basis of Classification

Learn the core NCERT criteria used to distinguish the major groups of plants.

🎯 Learning Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to classify major plant groups using body organisation, vascular tissue, seed formation and reproductive structures.

📖 Simple Explanation

Plant groups differ mainly in the complexity of their body and reproductive adaptations. A useful classification approach is to ask four questions:

  1. What is the plant body like?
  2. Are vascular tissues present?
  3. Are seeds formed?
  4. Are flowers or other reproductive structures present?

🔑 Key Concepts

  • Algae: generally simple thalloid body organisation.
  • Bryophytes: non-vascular plants that reproduce by spores.
  • Pteridophytes: vascular plants that reproduce by spores.
  • Gymnosperms: vascular seed plants with naked seeds.
  • Angiosperms: flowering plants with seeds enclosed in fruits.

⭐ Important NCERT Facts

  • Xylem and phloem are the major vascular tissues.
  • Seed formation separates seed plants from spore-producing groups.
  • Gymnosperms have naked seeds.
  • Angiosperms form flowers and fruits.

💡 Example-Based Thinking

Example: If a plant has vascular tissue but no seeds and reproduces through spores, classify it as a pteridophyte.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Do not assume that every vascular plant produces seeds. Pteridophytes are vascular but seedless.

📝 Quick Recap

Body organisation → Vascular tissue → Seeds → Flowers/fruits

Algae → Bryophytes → Pteridophytes → Gymnosperms → Angiosperms
