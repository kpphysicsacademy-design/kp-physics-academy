# SkillUp Mastery Practice Standard

## Mandatory structure for every concept practice/test
1. One question at a time.
2. Shuffle answer options while preserving the correct-answer mapping.
3. Show validation and explanation after every answer.
4. Wrong answers do **not** increase mastery progress.
5. Only a first successful mastery of a question increases overall progress.
6. At the end of a round, re-ask every incorrectly answered question.
7. Repeat review rounds until every question is correct.
8. Completion is allowed only at **100% mastery**.
9. Completion summary must show:
   - 100% mastery
   - questions mastered
   - first-pass accuracy
   - review rounds
   - skill progress
   - weak areas based on incorrect attempts
   - review concept and practice again actions
10. Use the shared files `skillup-mastery.css` and `skillup-mastery.js` for all new concept practice pages.

This standard applies to Science, Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, NEET, JEE and future concept practice pages.
