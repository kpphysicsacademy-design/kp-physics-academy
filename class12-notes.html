Class 12 Physics — Cheat Sheet

Formula-first revision • Board + competitive preparation

⚡ Electrostatics

F = (1/4πε₀) q₁q₂/r²
E = F/q = (1/4πε₀)q/r²
V = (1/4πε₀)q/r
C = Q/V

🔋 Current Electricity

I = Q/t
V = IR
R = ρL/A
P = VI = I²R = V²/R

For resistors in series: R_eq = ΣR. In parallel: 1/R_eq = Σ(1/R).

🧲 Moving Charges & Magnetism

F = q(v × B)
F = BIL sinθ
B_wire = μ₀I/(2πr)
τ = nIAB sinθ

🔄 EMI & AC

ε = −dΦ/dt
Φ = BA cosθ
V_rms = V₀/√2
I_rms = I₀/√2

Lenz's law gives the direction of induced current so that it opposes the change in magnetic flux.

📡 Electromagnetic Waves

c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀)
c = fλ

All electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed in vacuum but differ in frequency, wavelength and energy.

🔭 Ray & Wave Optics

Mirror: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
Lens: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
n = c/v
Fringe width β = λD/d

⚛️ Dual Nature

E = hν = hc/λ
λ = h/p
K_max = hν − φ

Photoelectric emission supports the particle nature of light. Matter waves are associated with moving particles.

☢️ Atoms & Nuclei

E_n = −13.6/n² eV for hydrogen
E = mc²
N = N₀e^(−λt)
T₁/₂ = 0.693/λ

💻 Semiconductor Electronics

Diode → mainly one-way conduction
p-type → holes majority carriers
n-type → electrons majority carriers

A p–n junction is the basic building block of many semiconductor devices. Learn diode characteristics and basic digital logic gates.
