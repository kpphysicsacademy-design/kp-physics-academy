Lesson 8 • Biological Classification

🌿 Lichens

Main concept: Lichens are symbiotic associations between an algal partner and a fungal partner. The alga prepares food by photosynthesis, while the fungus provides shelter and helps absorb water and minerals.

📚 Learn Lichens in Detail

🤝 1. Symbiotic Association
A lichen represents a close association between two different organisms. The two partners live together and both contribute to the functioning of the association.
🌱 2. Algal Partner
The algal partner is called the phycobiont. It performs photosynthesis and prepares organic food for the lichen association.
🍄 3. Fungal Partner
The fungal partner is called the mycobiont. It provides shelter and helps in absorbing water and minerals from the surroundings.
🏠 4. Mutual Benefit
The photosynthetic partner supplies food, while the fungal partner provides protection, support and improved access to water and minerals. This is a classic example of symbiosis.
🌍 5. Habitat
Lichens can grow on rocks, tree bark and other exposed surfaces. They are capable of surviving in a variety of environmental conditions.
🌫️ 6. Air Pollution Indicators
Lichens are sensitive to atmospheric pollution and are commonly used as indicators of air quality. Their presence or absence can provide information about environmental conditions.
🔬 7. Importance
Lichens contribute to ecological processes and are important examples for understanding symbiotic relationships in biology.
