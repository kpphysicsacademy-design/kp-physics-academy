Taxonomic Categories

Organisms are arranged in a hierarchy from species to kingdom.

1
Learn step by step
🧩

Species

Species is the basic unit of classification.

👥

Genus and Family

Related species form a genus and related genera form a family.

🏛️

Higher categories

Related families form orders, orders form classes and classes form higher groups.

👑

Hierarchy

Species → Genus → Family → Order → Class → Phylum/Division → Kingdom.

💡 REMEMBER: Moving upward in the hierarchy generally increases the number of organisms and decreases shared characters.
