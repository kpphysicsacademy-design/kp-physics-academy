🔄 CONCEPT 7 • PLANT KINGDOM

Plant Life Cycles & Alternation of Generations

Understand haploid and diploid phases, spores, gametes and the major patterns of plant life cycles.

🎯 Learning Objective

Explain alternation of generations and distinguish haplontic, diplontic and haplo-diplontic life cycles.

🖼️ Visual Learning

The life cycle alternates between haploid gametophyte and diploid sporophyte phases.

📖 Simple Explanation

Plants can have multicellular haploid and/or diploid phases. The gametophyte (n) produces gametes. Fertilisation forms the diploid zygote, which develops into the sporophyte (2n). Meiosis in the sporophyte produces haploid spores.

🔑 Core Cycle

GAMETOPHYTE (n) → GAMETES → FERTILISATION → ZYGOTE (2n) → SPOROPHYTE (2n) → MEIOSIS → SPORES (n) → GAMETOPHYTE

🧩 Major Life-Cycle Patterns

Haplontic

Main vegetative phase is haploid. The diploid phase is represented mainly by the zygote.

Diplontic

Main plant body is diploid; the haploid phase is restricted to gametes.

Haplo-diplontic

Both multicellular haploid and diploid phases occur.

Alternation

Relative dominance of gametophyte and sporophyte differs among plant groups.

🌿 Group Connection

  • Bryophytes: gametophyte is dominant; sporophyte is dependent.
  • Pteridophytes: dominant independent sporophyte; gametophyte is the prothallus.
  • Seed plants: sporophyte is dominant and gametophytes are highly reduced.

⚠️ NEET Traps

  • Spore is not the same as a gamete.
  • Meiosis produces spores in the sporophytic generation.
  • Fertilisation restores the diploid chromosome number.
  • Dominance of generations differs between plant groups.

💡 Example-Based Thinking

Question: Which phase produces gametes?

Answer: The gametophyte, because it is the haploid gamete-producing generation.

📝 Quick Recap

Gametophyte → gametes → fertilisation → sporophyte → meiosis → spores → gametophyte.
