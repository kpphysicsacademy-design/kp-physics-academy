🧩 Advanced 1D Motion

Learn → Practice → Retry → Master

🔥 0⭐ 0 XP❤️ 5

🧠 Learn

Advanced one-dimensional motion problems often involve piecewise motion, stopping conditions, turning points, or acceleration that changes with time or position. Choose a sign convention and apply definitions carefully rather than using constant-acceleration equations automatically.

Key idea: Always start from v = dx/dt and a = dv/dt when acceleration is not constant.
